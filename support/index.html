Help & support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email bakir.talibov@gmail.com, or in Rollvana open Settings → Send feedback. We usually reply within two business days.

I deleted a photo by mistake

Every delete goes through iOS. Open the Photos app → Collections → Recently Deleted and tap Recover. Photos stay there for 30 days.

I forgot a vault PIN

On the Vault screen tap “Forgot PIN?” and enter the recovery key you saved when you made your first vault. You can then set a new PIN in that vault’s settings. Without the recovery key we can’t open a vault for you — nobody can, by design.

I got a new iPhone

Restore your iPhone backup, open the Vault tab, tap “Forgot PIN?” and enter your recovery key. Your vaults open and you can set PINs again.

Where is the Vault locking setting?

Open a vault → the gear (Vault settings) → Auto-lock. Choose 15 seconds, 30 seconds, 1 minute, 5 minutes, or “When I leave the app”.

Why is a photo marked “Maybe” out of focus?

It looks a little soft but might be on purpose (portrait blur, motion). Maybe photos are never marked for you — you decide.
