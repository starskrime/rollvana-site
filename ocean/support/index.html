Help & support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email my99centsapps@gmail.com. We usually reply within two business days.

A site looks broken

Tap the shield in the address bar to turn Shields off for that site, then reload. Ocean remembers your choice for that site.

How do I make a mini app?

Open the site, tap ⋯ in the address bar and choose Make Mini App. It appears in Tide and on the start page.

A mini app asks me to sign in again

Mini apps start with the sign-ins you have in the browser. Some sites keep their sign-in elsewhere and ask once; after that the mini app remembers it.

My mini apps don’t appear on my other device

Check Settings → iCloud on both devices: iCloud sync must be on and you must be signed in to the same Apple Account. Tap Sync Now to sync right away.

How do I see the desktop version of a site?

Tap ⋯ in the address bar and choose Desktop Site. Ocean remembers it for that site.
