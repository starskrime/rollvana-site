Privacy policy

Short version: Ocean doesn’t collect, sell or share your data. Your history, tabs and sign-ins stay on your device, and we can’t see them.

Effective November 8, 2026 · Applies to the Ocean app for iPhone and iPad.

What we collect

Nothing. Ocean has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs, and it sends no data to us.

What stays on your device

  • Browsing history, tabs and site settings. Stored only on your device. You can clear history at any time in Settings.
  • Cookies and sign-ins. Kept by iOS’s web engine on your device. Each mini app has its own storage; deleting a mini app deletes its data.
  • Private tabs keep nothing after you close them.

iCloud

If iCloud sync is on (Settings → iCloud), your mini apps and playlists sync through your own private iCloud database, run by Apple. We have no access to it. You can turn sync off at any time.

Apple Intelligence

Summaries, translation, “Ask this page” and smart history search use Apple’s on-device models. Some requests may be handled by Apple’s Private Cloud Compute, which Apple designed so that your data is not stored or accessible to anyone, including Apple. Nothing is sent to us.

Services Ocean contacts

  • The websites you visit, as with any browser. Their own privacy policies apply.
  • Search suggestions come from your chosen search engine (DuckDuckGo by default) as you type in the address bar.
  • Site icons are fetched from DuckDuckGo’s icon service using the site’s address (not your identity).
  • Blocklists are updated from EasyList (easylist.to).
  • Ocean VPN, if you turn it on, sends your traffic through Cloudflare’s WARP network. Cloudflare’s privacy policy applies to that connection.

Children

Ocean is a general-purpose web browser and isn’t directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and its effective date.
