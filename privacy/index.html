Privacy policy

Short version: your photos, faces and vaults stay on your iPhone. We don’t collect, sell or share your data. We can’t see it.

Effective October 28, 2026 · Applies to the Rollvana app.

What we collect

Nothing. Rollvana has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs, and it sends no data to us.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Photos and videos. Rollvana reads your library (with your permission) to show, analyse, edit and organise it. Analysis — blur, duplicates, text, scenes and faces — runs on your device.
  • Faces. People groups faces with an on-device model. Face data is stored only on your iPhone, is never uploaded, and is never used for advertising or data mining. Deleting the app deletes it.
  • Vaults. Photos you move into a vault are encrypted on your iPhone with Apple’s CryptoKit. Your PIN and recovery key are never sent anywhere; we have no way to open a vault or reset a PIN.
  • Face ID. Handled entirely by iOS. Rollvana only learns whether you were recognised, never your face data.
  • Camera, microphone and speech. Used only when you take a photo, record a voiceover or create captions. Speech is transcribed on your device.

Apple services

To show place names (like “Shibuya”) and Places on a map, Rollvana asks Apple Maps to look up a photo’s location. Apple receives that location, not the photo, under Apple’s privacy policy. Purchases are handled by the App Store.

Deleting photos

Rollvana never deletes on its own: every delete goes through the iOS confirmation and into Recently Deleted for 30 days.

Children

Rollvana doesn’t collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes we’ll update this page and the date above. Questions: in Rollvana, open Settings → Send feedback.
